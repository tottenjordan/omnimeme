# Note: Gemini Omni Flash Video Directing Best Practices

## Overview
Gemini Omni Flash preview supports multimodal inputs (text, image, audio) and video generation capabilities. To achieve cinematic output quality, prompt engineering for Omni Flash requires structured directing best practices.

## Prompt Structuring Taxonomy
1. **Subject Description**: Detailed physical characteristics, subject actions, clothing, expression.
2. **Action & Motion Dynamics**: Speeds, directional movements, frame pacing, subject-camera interactions.
3. **Camera Directing**: Shot size (close-up, wide shot, aerial), movement (pan, tilt, tracking, orbit, steadycam), lens choices (e.g. 35mm, macro, telephoto).
4. **Lighting & Atmosphere**: Natural or artificial lighting (golden hour, volumetric rays, neon noir, high-key), weather, particle details.
5. **Style & Aesthetics**: Genre (photorealistic 35mm film, anime, 3D render, documentary), color palette, texture.
6. **Audio & Sound Cues**: Ambient audio, sound effects timing, voiceover tone if multimodal audio/video sync is targeted.

## Omni Flash Specific Prompting Insights
- **Avoid Ambiguity**: Use specific, physical descriptions rather than vague adjectives (e.g. "slow cinematic tracking shot following a runner at golden hour" vs "nice video").
- **Motion Control**: Specify speed and trajectory clearly to maintain temporal consistency.
- **Negative Prompting / Constraints**: State explicit constraints (e.g., no sudden cuts, single continuous shot).
